# Intellify IT

**We build the systems that run your business: custom software, web and mobile apps, automation and AI, secured from day one.**

Based in Nairobi, Kenya, since 2024. We serve clients across Kenya, Africa and worldwide.

## What we do
- **Custom systems & automation:** business systems built around how you work, with the busywork automated.
- **Web & mobile apps:** web apps, mobile apps and customer portals that are fast, secure and easy to use.
- **AI solutions:** assistants that answer from your documents, document AI, and AI inside your products and workflows.
- **Security & compliance:** AI-assisted security testing and compliance work, backed by our team's ISO/IEC 27001 credentials.
- **Cloud, hosting & domains:** domains, hosting, email and SSL set up, secured and renewed for you.

## Our own products
AGIE (AI governance) and Budget Book (personal finance) are in development; AI Lab is a concept. See [intellify-it.com/products](https://intellify-it.com/products/).

## How we build
Every change goes through an issue, a failing test first, one gate (`bin/check`) and an independent review of the exact commit before it merges. Secrets never live in git.
